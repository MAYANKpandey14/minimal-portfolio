# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Business owners, startup founders, agency leads, and clients looking for a high-end freelance web designer and full-stack developer to create fast, conversion-focused websites and applications.

## Product Purpose
Position Mayank Pandey as an elite, high-performance web designer & full-stack engineer. Showcase client projects with interactive detail, convey trust and authority, and convert traffic into leads.

## Brand Personality
Sleek, authoritative, high-performance, conversion-driven, precise.

## Anti-references
- Repetitive, generic SaaS bento-grid cards on every section.
- Cluttered visual noise and low-contrast body copy.
- Gimmicky hand-drawn SVGs or template-y layout tropes.

## Design Principles
1. Show craft and technical polish through bespoke, interactive components rather than repetitive grid cards.
2. Maintain high readability, clear typography hierarchy, and purposeful subtle motion.
3. Guide the visitor cleanly toward client work results, interactive live demos, and clear contact actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast compliance, responsive layout across all device sizes, reduced motion support.
